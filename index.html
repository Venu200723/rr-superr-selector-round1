How the picks strengthen RR

RR's needs come from public reporting on the 2026 season (sources on each card). Pressure stats are from this analysis, split by phase.

Methodology in brief

Pressure Index (0–1, every ball)
  • Chase difficulty (0.50): required run rate vs a par of 8.5, plus how few wickets and balls are left. Uses DLS-revised targets where present.
  • Death overs (0.25): overs 16–20, with partial weight for overs 14–15.
  • Collapse (0.15): wickets in the last 12 balls and few wickets in hand.
  • Chase × death interaction (0.10), plus a +0.18 boost for knockout matches.
  • A ball is high-pressure if its index is in the top 25% (≥ ).
Data checks
  • runs is runs off the bat only. A ball's total is runs + extras1_runs + extras_2_runs. Rebuilt scores match 397 of 400 non-DLS results.
  • Bowler wickets count Caught, Bowled, LBW, Stumped and Hit Wicket. Run-outs, retirements and obstructing the field are excluded.
Scoring
    • Rates are shrunk toward the population mean using a prior worth 50 balls (empirical Bayes), so small samples can't top the list.
    • Phase-adjusted: runs above or below the league average for that innings and over, among high-pressure balls.
    • IPL balls are weighted ×1.35, Full-Member T20I balls ×1.00 and other T20 balls ×0.70.
